How To Play

Find groups of four items that share something in common.

  • Select four items and tap Submit to check if your guess is correct.
  • Find all four groups without making 4 mistakes!

Category Examples

  • Stanford residence halls: Roble, Branner, Lagunita, Wilbur
  • Hoover ___: Tower, Dam, Vacuum, Institution

Categories will always be more specific than “5-letter words,” “names” or “verbs.”

Each puzzle has exactly one solution. Watch out for items that seem to belong to multiple categories!
